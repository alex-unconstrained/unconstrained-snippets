# Where the great schools are now: international schools and Brazil/LatAm context, 2023–2026
*Research brief for the UnconstrainED framework. First client: International School of Curitiba (ISC). Compiled 24 Sep 2026.*

**Method note:** I checked every claim below with WebSearch/WebFetch against the named source. Several primary sites blocked automated fetching (ibo.org, aassa.com, state.gov, loc.gov, planalto.gov.br and some gov.br pages returned 403 or 503). For those claims I used reputable secondary coverage and say so. The session's web-search budget ran out before I could look deeper into AASSA, ISB Brussels, ASB Bombay and Graded, so those appear only where I could verify something. Evidence ratings: **Strong** means a primary source or official document. **Emerging** means announced or piloted with limited outcome data, or reported only by secondary sources. **Anecdotal** means marketing, self-report, or one secondary source.

## 0. Baseline: what ISC is (verified)
- Founded in 1959 as the American Cooperative School. It offers the IB Diploma in Grades 11–12. ISC lists accreditation by the Paraná State Secretariat of Education, CIS, NEASC and the IB. It names six core competencies: communication, collaboration, creativity, critical thinking, character and citizenship. It uses MAP Growth. Source: https://iscbrazil.com/about-us/ (accessed Sep 2026). **Strong** (self-reported).
- Wikipedia describes three diploma pathways: an American diploma for all students, a Brazilian diploma for Brazilian citizens, and the optional IB. It also lists AASSA membership and older AdvancED accreditation (since 1984). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_of_Curitiba. **Emerging.** Please confirm with ISC. Its site lists CIS and NEASC, not AdvancED/Cognia.
- ISC reports the Class of 2025 received acceptances from 171 universities in 11 countries. Source: doris.school profile, https://www.doris.school/schools/brazil/international-school-of-curitiba (2025/26). **Anecdotal.**

**Why it matters:** ISC is accredited by both CIS and NEASC, is an IB school, and is regulated in Brazil. So the CIS 2024 framework, the NEASC ACE principles, IB AI and assessment policy, and Brazil's new AI rules for schools all apply to it directly.

## 1. Themes with named exemplars

### Theme A: Accreditors are moving from compliance to "learning-centred, school-led" improvement
- **CIS International Accreditation Framework (announced 8 Nov 2024).** It has three dimensions: Guiding Principles, Learning and Well-being. It adds "Cornerstone Expectations" (Purpose, Practices and Planning), "guided choice" so schools lead development in areas they choose, smaller visiting teams, and ongoing CIS support instead of a one-off evaluation. https://www.cois.org/about-cis/news/post/~board/news/post/announcement-thenew-cis-international-accreditation-framework-process. **Strong.**
- **CIS 2025 year-in-review.** Schools reported more "choice, agency, and relevance" under the new model. CIS also launched an International Safeguarding Development Programme (eight sessions over a year for safeguarding leads), an Educational Transitions Framework with a "Foundations of Transitions Care" course, and socially responsible leadership programmes for leaders and governing boards. Its student-agency resources were the most-accessed resource among counsellors in 2025. https://www.cois.org/about-cis/news/post/~board/perspectives-blog/post/how-we-shaped-the-future-of-international-education-in-2025 (late 2025). **Strong** (self-reported by CIS).
- **NEASC ACE Learning.** It has ten Learning Principles, including Learning Purposes, Dimensions of Learning, Learner Autonomy & Engagement, Research/Reflection/Action, and Inclusion, Diversity, Equity & Belonging. It runs a five-year cycle and uses the "4 Cs" (Conceptual Understanding, Commitment, Capacity, Competence) to diagnose what drives or blocks change. Its core ask is that each school write its own "Shared Understanding of High-Quality Learning." https://www.neasc.org/accreditation/pathways/international/ace-lp-resources and https://www.neasc.org/resources-members/resources-international/ace-learning-principles (accessed Sep 2026). Some NEASC pages returned 404, so I could not confirm the date of the latest (2.0) revision. **Strong.**
- **Exemplar: Western Academy of Beijing (WAB).** WAB publishes a public page showing how it uses the ACE 2.0 Learning Principles to measure its impact: https://learn.wab.edu/faculty/impact/ace (undated). **Emerging.**

### Theme B: Assessing and credentialing competencies beyond grades
- **CIS × Melbourne Metrics, "New Metrics for International Schools" (announced 22 Nov 2024).** The programme uses standards-based (not standardised) teacher-judgement tools on seven competencies: Agency in Learning, Collaboration, Communication, Acting Ethically, Quality Thinking, Active Citizenship and Personal Development. The aim is a "complementary assessment record" for university entry. https://www.cois.org/about-cis/news/post/~board/news/post/developing-new-metrics-for-learner-success-a-new-program-in-partnership-with-melbourne-metrics ; https://education.unimelb.edu.au/melbourne-metrics/home/new-metrics-international-schools-program. CIS reports that 23 schools in 13 countries have joined (CIS 2025 review). **Emerging:** pilot stage, no published outcomes yet.
- **Exemplar: WAB joined the pilot (31 Oct 2025).** It is a two-year pilot tied to WAB's "Profiles of WAB Alumni" and IB ATL skills, run through its "Collaboration for Impact" professional learning model. https://www.wab-edu.cn/whats-happening/learning-news/view-page/~board/news/post/melbourne-metrics. **Emerging.**
- **Mastery Transcript Consortium.** About 400 schools; ETS acquired it in May 2024. CIS lists MTC as a supporting member. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mastery_Transcript_Consortium ; https://www.cois.org/about-cis/news/post/~board/news/post/new-supporting-member-mastery-transcript-consortium-mtc. **Emerging.** I did not verify which Latin American schools are members.
- **Parallel network move: Nord Anglia "Learner Ambitions."** Six ambitions (critical, creative, committed, curious, compassionate, collaborative) were piloted in 27 schools. Boston College is researching the network's metacognition work, with results expected in 2025. https://www.nordangliaeducation.com/news/2024/05/24/nord-anglia-education-publishes-new-insights-on-the-role-of-ai-and-metacognition-in-learning (24 May 2024). **Emerging.**

### Theme C: IB, the anchor for AI policy and assessment change
- **IB AI stance (from March 2023).** The IB will not ban AI. AI-generated content must be credited in the text and in the bibliography, and AI guidance was added as an appendix to the Academic Integrity Policy. The IB states the test this way: "Did the student use AI to help them learn? If so, then it is acceptable," versus using it "to pretend they did something they did not." A 2024 IB resource evaluates 13 scenarios of AI use in coursework. Sources: ibo.org statement page (https://ibo.org/news/news-about-the-ib/statement-from-the-ib-about-chatgpt-and-artificial-intelligence-in-assessment-and-education/, blocked, so I verified it through ISB Beijing's guide https://libguides.isb.cn/teachai, updated 16 Sep 2026). **Strong.**
- **Extended Essay redesign.** The new guide launched in February 2025, with first assessment in May 2027. All EEs must be authenticated by both the student and the supervisor. https://ibo.org/university-admission/latest-curriculum-updates/dp-extended-essay-updates/ (blocked; confirmed via search snippet and CASIE, https://www.casieonline.org/post/the-updated-ib-extended-essay-guide-new-criteria-full-writing-roadmap/). **Strong.**
- **Digital (on-screen) DP exams.** A limited first rollout starts in May 2026 in selected schools, beginning with Language A and B. Paper and digital results are set to be comparable, more subject groups follow after the trials, and full digital delivery is expected by the early 2030s. https://ibo.org/programmes/diploma-programme/assessment-and-exams/digital-examinations-for-the-dp-and-cp/ (blocked); https://www.aacrao.org/edge/emergent-news/ib-launches-online-ibdp-exams-in-2026/ ; https://www.ibnews.org/2025/12/digital-exams-are-coming-to-ib-diploma-what-every-student-should-know/ (Dec 2025). **Strong/Emerging:** the direction is confirmed, but the timeline for later subjects is not.
- **Exemplar: International School of Beijing library guides.** ISB publishes AI and academic-integrity guidance for teachers and a dedicated guide on AI in the Extended Essay (https://libguides.isb.cn/extendedessay/usingai). Low-cost, public guidance like this is typical of schools that are ahead. **Strong** (primary).

### Theme D: AI adoption. The best schools go slow with young children, deep with teachers, and literate for everyone
- **UWCSEA (Singapore).** It publishes a technology approach for each age band. K1–G2 use school iPads under direct supervision. Middle school introduces home-owned MacBooks. Phones: none on campus for K1–G5, and kept in bags during learning time from G6–12. Student use of generative AI is "extremely limited until at least the end of Middle School." AI literacy sits inside a Digital & Information Literacy curriculum, with a "WISE" framework meant to make sure students improve their learning rather than outsource it. https://www.uwcsea.edu.sg/learning/technology-at-uwcsea (accessed Sep 2026); interview with the Heads of Digital Learning, https://www.relocatemagazine.com/how-uwcseas-k-12-ai-approach-prepares-students-for-the-future-iesfau25 (13 Nov 2025). **Strong** (primary policy); outcomes not published.
- **Cognita (network, about 95,000 students).** "Cognita AI" launched on 30 June 2025. It combines a Flint partnership (a personalised AI tutor, rolled out network-wide after pilots in six schools in Spain, Mexico, Vietnam, the UAE and the UK), teacher professional learning, student AI-literacy curricula, family engagement resources, and safeguards with teacher oversight. https://www.cognita.com/news-and-views/launch-of-cognita-ai-global-roll-out-of-cutting-edge-ai-platform-to-support-teaching-learning/ ; https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250630359420/en/. **Emerging:** pilot outcomes are described only in general terms.
- **GEMS Education (UAE, about 50 schools).** On 20 May 2025 it announced subject-specific AI agents for differentiation, formative assessment and planning. Pilots are running, with wider rollout in 2025–26. It is also running Microsoft Copilot training for leaders, AI literacy for all staff and for students aged 13+, and Arabic-language tools. https://www.tes.com/magazine/news/specialist-sector/gems-major-ai-push-teacher-training-curricula. It later announced "AI teacher twins" with Napster, targeted before the end of 2026: https://gulfnews.com/uae/uaes-gems-education-to-roll-out-ai-avatars-of-teachers-across-its-schools-1.500683733. **Emerging.** The teacher-avatar model is contested and is not a recommended benchmark for ISC.
- **Nord Anglia.** British International School Kuala Lumpur built an AI teaching assistant; network publications cover AI and metacognition, with UCL Knowledge Lab (Rose Luckin) involved. https://www.nordangliaeducation.com/news/2024/05/07/nord-anglia-schools-spearheading-artificial-intelligence-in-education (7 May 2024). **Anecdotal/Emerging.**
- **Regional signal: Tri-Association (Central America, Mexico, Colombia, Caribbean).** The 2026 conference (1 Oct 2026) includes "H-AI-H: Using Our Most Human Traits to Navigate a GenAI World" (Myron Dueck), "The AI Conversation Every School Should Be Having" (Faria), and "AI-Proofing PBL Projects" (International School of Sosua). https://www.tri-association.org/conference-pd/annual-educators-conference/conference-program. The 2025 programme included "Preparing Students for an Ethical AI Future" (The Columbus School) per search results. **Anecdotal:** this shows what peers are working on, not outcomes.
- **Not verified:** I could not verify AASSA-specific AI initiatives (site blocked), or current AI programmes at ISB Brussels, ASB Bombay or Graded. ASB's site claims "AI-enhanced learning" but gives little concrete detail (https://www.asbindia.org/life-at-asb/our-campuses/technology). **Anecdotal.** Do not cite these schools to the Board as AI exemplars without further checking.

- UWCSEA's phone policy is described in Theme D. Brazil now bans phones by law (see §3), which puts ISC on the same footing as the leading schools. **Strong.**

The strategic plan I found, "A Commitment to Every Student's Success" (Be Successful, Be Happy, Be Involved), may be out of date. ## 2. What separates genuinely leading international schools from the rest
This is synthesis across the evidence above. Treat it as analytical judgement, not proven fact.
1. **They define learning first, then choose technology.** WAB uses ACE principles and alumni profiles, Nord Anglia has Learner Ambitions, UWCSEA has Mission Competencies. AI policy follows from each school's definition of learning, not the other way round.
2. **Their AI rules differ by age and are published.** UWCSEA shows each age band's rules. Brazil's new CNE rules require the same approach (see §3).
3. **They make evidence of learning visible beyond grades.** Examples are New Metrics, MTC, and redesigned authentication (IB EE sign-off, process evidence).
4. **They invest in teachers before tools.** Cognita and GEMS both pair platforms with staff professional learning and AI literacy. The leaders run pilots with evaluation before rolling out.
5. **They treat safeguarding and data as strategy, not IT.** Examples are CIS safeguarding leads, UWCSEA's e-Safety Committee, and Brazil's LGPD/ECA Digital rules.
6. **They publish their policies.** Public guides such as ISB Beijing's library guides and UWCSEA's technology page build family trust and hold the school to account.
7. **They use accreditation as their strategic engine.** CIS "guided choice" and NEASC ACE reward schools that link accreditation to their strategic plan instead of running them in parallel.

## 3. Brazil / Latin America policy and market context

**AI governance for schools (the most important new fact for ISC):**
- **CNE national guidelines on AI in education**, approved on **1 Sep 2026**, apply to public **and private** institutions at every level. Provisions as reported:
  - Human oversight is required.
  - AI may not replace teachers.
  - No automated decisions on grades, promotion, retention, certification or discipline.
  - No autonomous or unsupervised student access to generative or conversational AI in early childhood and the early primary years; use must be planned and teacher-mediated.
  - No fully automated essay marking, and AI-detector results may not be the sole basis for punishment.
  - Schools must set internal data-governance rules.
  - 12 months to adapt.

  Sources: Porvir https://porvir.org/cne-diretrizes-inteligencia-artificial-escola-universidade/ (Sep 2026); https://blog.fernandobarra.com.br/cne-regras-ia-escolas-universidades/. The second source says MEC ratification (homologação) is **pending** and describes a four-tier risk classification. **Emerging.** It is very recent, secondary sources differ on some details, and I have not seen the final text. ISC should get legal review of the official resolution once it is ratified.
- **MEC Referencial for the responsible development and use of AI in education** (early 2026, Feb–Mar; sources differ). It sets seven principles and risk tiers: low, moderate (tutors, writing assistance), high (automated marking, biometrics) and prohibited (AI deciding student progression). Guidance by stage: not recommended in early childhood except for inclusion, then progressive AI literacy in primary, then ethics and societal impact in secondary. Its two axes are learning "with" AI and "about" AI. A companion document, "IA na Educação Básica: Caminhos para o Currículo e a Prática Docente," followed in April 2026. Sources: https://jeduca.org.br/noticia/ia-na-educacao-entenda-o-novo-referencial-do-mec-e-pontos-de-atencao ; https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/referencial-de-ia-na-educacao ; https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/escolas-conectadas/documentos/ia-educacao-basica.pdf. **Strong/Emerging.** Jeduca cites 64% of Brazilian teachers reporting they lack the knowledge to teach with AI.
- **PBIA 2024–2028, "IA para o Bem de Todos"** (launched in 2024; IDS article dated 15 Aug 2024). Planned investment is R$23.03bn. Education actions include AI-personalised maths, AI attendance monitoring against dropout ("Gestão Presente"), and AI training. https://ids.org.br/en/news-post/brazils-federal-government-launches-the-brazilian-artificial-intelligence-plan-2024-2028/ ; plan PDF https://conteudo.sidi.org.br/hubfs/202409%20PBIA%202024-2028%20IA%20Para%20o%20Bem%20de%20Todos.pdf. **Strong.** It is mostly focused on the public system.

**Curriculum:**
- **BNCC Computação.** The complement to the national curriculum was set by Parecer CNE/CEB 2/2022 and Resolução CNE/CEB 1/2022 (4 Oct 2022). Implementation starts from 2023, and networks may teach it across subjects or as a separate subject. Conviva (a support platform for municipal education secretariats) reports 2025 as the adaptation year and 2026 as mandatory implementation for public networks. Sources: https://undime.org.br/noticia/07-10-2022-10-59-mec-aprova-parecer-que-define-normas-sobre-o-ensino-de-computacao-na-educacao-basica ; https://www.lex.com.br/resolucao-cne-ceb-no-1-de-4-de-outubro-de-2022/ ; https://convivaeducacao.org.br/fique_atento/5768. **Strong.** How it applies to a private international school depends on the Paraná state system. **My inference, not verified:** because ISC issues a Brazilian diploma, its Brazilian curriculum must reflect the BNCC, including Computação. Confirm with ISC's Brazilian pedagogical director.

**Bilingual regulation:**
- The national guidelines for plurilingual/bilingual education (**Parecer CNE/CEB 2/2020**, approved 9 Jul 2020) define a bilingual school, minimum instruction time in the additional language, and teacher qualifications. As of the latest sources I found, they were **still awaiting MEC ratification.** https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/cne/pdf/normas-classificadas-por-assunto/diretrizes-para-a-educacao-basica/pceb002_20.pdf ; https://www.gov.br/mec/pt-br/cne/normas-classificadas-por-assunto/educacao-plurilingue. **Strong** on the text; **unverified** whether ratification happened in 2025–26. Some state councils, such as Minas Gerais (Parecer 381/2021), issued their own rules. I did not verify whether Paraná has.

**Data protection and child digital safety:**
- **LGPD Art. 14 and ANPD Enunciado CD/ANPD 1/2023 (22 May 2023).** Children's data may be processed under any legal basis in Arts. 7 and 11, as long as the child's best interest prevails. https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anpd-divulga-enunciado-sobre-o-tratamento-de-dados-pessoais-de-criancas-e-adolescentes. **Strong.**
- **ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025).** Signed 17 Sep 2025, in force **17 Mar 2026**. It requires privacy-by-default for services aimed at or "likely to be accessed" by minors, reliable age verification (self-declaration not allowed), limits on profiling children for advertising, a loot-box ban, and parental linking for social media under 16. The ANPD enforces it, with fines up to 10% of Brazilian revenue, and ANPD implementing rules are still pending. https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2023-2026/2025/lei/l15211.htm ; https://www.dataprivacybr.org/eca-digital-entra-em-vigor-o-que-a-lei-preve-e-o-que-ainda-falta-regulamentar/. **Strong.** The obligations fall mainly on providers, but they directly affect which edtech and AI vendors ISC can use.
- **Phone law (Lei 15.100/2025, 13 Jan 2025).** It restricts personal electronic devices during class, recess and breaks in public **and private** basic-education schools. Exceptions cover teaching purposes, accessibility, health, and danger or force majeure. https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2025-02-13/brazil-new-law-prohibits-cell-phone-use-in-schools/ ; https://www.cnn.com/2025/01/13/americas/brazil-restricts-smartphones-in-schools-intl-latam. **Strong.**

**State context (Paraná, ISC's home state):**
- The Paraná education secretariat (SEED-PR) partners with Google. It uses AI feedback on essays for about 120,000 third-year high-school students, an AI reading-fluency assessment ("Fluência Paraná") for up to about 125,000 second-graders, and plans a technical course in AI and Data in 32 state schools from 2026 (about 2,000 students). Sources: https://www.educacao.pr.gov.br/Noticia/Parana-firma-parceria-inedita-com-Google-para-ampliar-Inteligencia-Artificial-na-Educacao ; https://www.educacao.pr.gov.br/Noticia/Parana-sera-um-dos-primeiros-estados-do-Brasil-ofertar-curso-tecnico-de-IA-na-rede-publica ; https://www.parana.pr.gov.br/aen/Noticia/Conectividade-e-IA-nas-escolas-do-Parana-sao-destaques-no-Smart-City-Expo-2025 (2025). **Strong** as government self-report; the figures come from search snippets because the pages returned 503. **Implication:** Curitiba families see heavy, visible AI use in the public system. Some of that use, such as automated essay feedback, now sits in the "high-risk" tier of the national frameworks. ISC can set itself apart through AI use that is more human-supervised and more principled, not simply through more AI.
- **São Paulo state caution.** Plans to have AI generate lesson slides for review by teachers drew academic criticism on quality and equity grounds: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666389924001533 (Patterns, 2024). **Emerging.**

**Market:**
- **ISC Research (Jan 2025).** Globally there are 14,833 international schools, 7.4m students, and US$67.3bn in fee income (up 22% since 2020). https://iscresearch.com/the-international-schools-market-in-2025/. **Strong.** A search snippet gave Brazil 438 international schools and 32% five-year growth. **Emerging:** I could not confirm this on the primary page.
- **ABEBI (Brazilian Association of Bilingual Education), via secondary sources.** Over 1,200 bilingual schools, about 3% of roughly 40,000 private schools. https://internationalschool.global/crescimento-das-escolas-bilingues-no-brasil-numeros-desafios-e-o-futuro-da-educacao/. **Anecdotal:** vendor blog.
- **Curitiba.** Competitors include Red House International School (two Curitiba units), Colégio Sesi Internacional and Deutsche Schule Curitiba. A Gazeta do Povo schools guide calls ISC the only school recognised as international in Curitiba: https://especiais.gazetadopovo.com.br/guia-escolas-e-matriculas/linhas-pedagogicas/ensino-bilingue/ ; https://redhouse.com.br/nossas-escolas/parana/curitiba-i/. **Anecdotal.** I found no Curitiba market-size data.

## 4. Shortlist to show the Board
1. **CIS's new accreditation framework (Nov 2024).** Guided choice and well-being as a dimension mean ISC's next CIS cycle can double as its strategic plan. **Strong.**
2. **NEASC ACE Learning Principles, with WAB as the worked example.** A school-wide definition of high-quality learning is the foundation. **Strong.**
3. **The IB's AI integrity test ("help them learn" vs "pretend"), plus EE authentication and digital exams from 2026.** These are the assessment changes ISC's DP students will face. **Strong.**
4. **UWCSEA's age-by-age technology and AI policy.** A clear public model that already fits Brazil's CNE ban on unsupervised generative AI in the early years. **Strong.**
5. **CIS × Melbourne New Metrics, and WAB's pilot.** Credentialing competencies. ISC's six competencies could be assessed this way. **Emerging.**
6. **Network AI rollouts: Cognita AI (pilot, then scale, with teacher PD and family engagement) versus GEMS AI teacher avatars.** A contrast in risk appetite. **Emerging.**
7. **Brazil's legal perimeter:** CNE AI guidelines (Sep 2026, 12 months to comply), the MEC Referencial, ECA Digital (in force Mar 2026), LGPD/ANPD 2023, and the phone law of 2025. These are non-negotiable, and a 12-month clock may already be running. **Strong/Emerging.**
8. **Paraná public-system AI (Google partnership).** Local context: parents will compare ISC with it. **Strong** (self-report).

## 5. Considerations specific to Brazil for ISC
- **Compliance deadline.** Plan AI policy, governance and data rules to meet the CNE 12-month window, counted from MEC ratification (to be confirmed). Watch for ratification.
- **Two regulators, one policy.** Line the IB academic-integrity rules up with CNE's ban on AI detectors as sole evidence for sanctions, and with the ban on automated grading or promotion decisions. Build one ISC AI policy that meets IB, CIS, NEASC and Brazilian rules together.
- **Vendor due diligence under LGPD and ECA Digital.** Keep an inventory of AI and edtech tools, set processing agreements, avoid sharing identifiable student data with external AI (a CNE recommendation as reported), and base decisions for under-18s on the child's best interest.
- **Early years.** No autonomous generative AI for young children. ECC and early elementary use should be teacher-mediated only, consistent with ISC's Reggio Emilia approach.
- **Brazilian diploma track.** Map BNCC Computação and the MEC stage-by-stage AI-literacy guidance into the Brazilian curriculum strand. Confirm Paraná state requirements.
- **Bilingual rules are unsettled.** Watch for ratification of Parecer 2/2020 and any Paraná state council rule. These may affect staffing (teacher qualification) and time allocation.
- **Market position.** In a fast-growing bilingual market, ISC's unusual status (IB, CIS and NEASC accreditation, American and Brazilian diplomas) is a differentiator. A principled, transparent approach to AI is a credible way to stand out against both bilingual franchises and the heavy AI use in Paraná's public system.

## 6. Signals for 2027 and beyond
- IB digital exams expand beyond languages, with full digital delivery by the early 2030s (**Emerging**). The first new-model Extended Essays are assessed in May 2027 (**Strong**).
- Competency credentials (New Metrics, MTC under ETS) may begin to be accepted by universities; results from the 2-year pilots are expected around 2027 (**Emerging**).
- Brazil: CNE AI guidelines take effect for schools around late 2027, with a 24-month review of the guidelines and glossary. ANPD's ECA Digital rules on age verification and parental tools are due. PBIA indicators are tracked on OBIA/NIC.br (https://obia.nic.br/indicadores-pbia) (**Emerging**).
- Networks are moving to AI agents and "teacher twins" (GEMS, targeted by the end of 2026). Expect parents to compare offerings and regulators to push back in Brazil's risk-tiered regime (**Emerging**).
- Regional associations (Tri-Association 2026 programme) are shifting from AI tools to how schools stay human and how to "AI-proof" project-based learning (**Anecdotal**).
