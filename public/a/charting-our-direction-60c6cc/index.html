01 · What you asked for

What you asked for, and how we heard it

You told us you want a partner to lead your strategic direction process, and that you don't want a traditional strategic plan: a binder that gets approved, filed, and quietly forgotten. We agree. Organizations move forward when they know where they're heading, why, and how they'll know they're getting there, and when their people recognize themselves in that direction.

[One or two lines echoing what Andreas and the Board said on the call, in their words: "You said..."]

We've kept this proposal short on purpose. The detail should come from you and your community, and we'd rather shape it with you than guess at it.

So we've designed a process that starts with listening, turns what we hear into clear choices for the Board, brings your community together in person to commit to those choices, and then stays with you while you act on them. The process moves between virtual and in-person work, and between live and asynchronous participation, so people can take part in the way that works for them.

04 · Frameworks

The frameworks we'll use, and why

We'll lean on four approaches, each chosen for a specific job in this process.

01 · Phase 1

Empathy interviews

From human-centered design. Open, story-based conversations surface what people actually experience day to day. That's the raw material a direction your people believe in has to be built from.

02 · Framing and April

Appreciative Inquiry and SOAR

Strengths, Opportunities, Aspirations, Results. Both start from what's already working, which keeps a Board conversation focused on where you want to go.

03 · Testing the direction

Three Horizons with futures thinking

We'll hold today's work, the transition, and the future you're building in one picture, and stress-test it against a few plausible futures, including how AI and emerging technologies may reshape schooling.

04 · Facilitation

Liberating Structures

Simple formats such as 1-2-4-All and 15% Solutions give every person in the room a real way in.

A question we'll bring to the Board early

[If the American School of Curitiba were starting from scratch today, knowing what AI can now do, what would you still choose to do exactly as you do now, and what would you never build again?]

05 · AI in this work

How we use AI in this work

UnconstrainED is an AI-in-education firm, and we use our own tools in our work. During this process our AI strategy agent works alongside our team in two ways.

Hear everyone properly

It helps us read and organize every interview note, focus-group record, and open-ended response. It groups themes, flags where groups disagree, and points out minority views that are easy to miss.

Keep the evidence trail

Every theme and recommendation we present can be traced back to what people actually said. When a Board member asks "where did this come from?", we can show them.

A strategy companion after April

We build the school its own agent, configured to your agreed direction and the full evidence behind it. Through the partnership year, leadership can ask it how a new decision fits the priorities, what the community said on a given issue, and where each priority stands, and it helps prepare progress updates for the Board. It stays yours when the partnership ends.

People stay in charge

The agent drafts and organizes; our team reads, checks, and interprets everything, and the conclusions are ours and yours. We'll agree a data-protection approach with you before any listening begins, covering what's collected, where it's stored, who sees it, and how identifying details are removed. We only use AI tools whose terms don't allow them to train on your data.
