01 · What you asked for

Not another binder

You want a partner to lead your strategic direction process, and you don't want a traditional strategic plan: a binder that gets approved, filed, and quietly forgotten.

We agree. Organizations move forward when they know where they're heading, why, and how they'll know they're getting there, and when their people recognize themselves in that direction.

[One or two lines echoing what the Head of School and the Board said on the call, in their words: "You said..."]

We've kept this proposal short on purpose. The detail should come from you and your community, and we'd rather shape it with you than guess at it.

Why Curitiba gets this

A city that planned with its people

Curitiba is known around the world for acting on a long-term direction. In 1972 it turned Rua XV de Novembro into one of Brazil's first pedestrian streets, and in 1974 it opened the bus network that cities everywhere still come to study. The lesson we take from it: a direction sticks when people can see themselves in it and watch it change their day-to-day.

That's the kind of direction we want to help ISC set.

The frameworks we'll use, and why

Four approaches, each chosen for a specific job in this process, plus a deliberate look outward.

01Listening

Empathy interviews

Open, story-based conversations surface what people actually experience day to day. That's the raw material for a direction your people believe in.

02Framing and April

Appreciative Inquiry and SOAR

Strengths, Opportunities, Aspirations, Results. Starting from what already works keeps the Board focused on where you want to go.

03Testing the direction

Three Horizons and futures thinking

Today, the transition, and the future in one picture, stress-tested against plausible futures, including how AI may reshape schooling.

04Facilitation

Liberating Structures

Simple formats such as 1-2-4-All and 15% Solutions give every person in the room a real way in.

And: looking outward

Beyond our own ecosystem

Alongside these four frameworks, we bring the world in. From our experience, our network, and our engagement with great schools around the world, we download for ISC's decision makers what leading schools are doing now, so they can see what makes sense and what is relevant to their strategic direction.

It works in both directions. Your community's wants and needs, hopes and fears take on more shape when they are set against what is out there. And what is out there only matters where it meets what your community actually said.

A question we'll bring to the Board early

If ISC were starting from scratch today, knowing what AI can now do, what would you still choose to do exactly as you do now, and what would you never build again?

04 · How we use AI

Hear everyone. Show the evidence.

UnconstrainED is an AI-in-education firm, and we use our own tools in our work. Our AI strategy agent helps us read and organize every interview note, focus-group record and open-ended response. It groups themes, flags where groups disagree, and surfaces minority views that are easy to miss.

Every theme and recommendation can be traced back to what people actually said. When a Board member asks "where did this come from?", we can show them.

After April, the school gets its own strategy companion, built on the agreed direction and the full evidence behind it. It stays yours when the partnership ends.
