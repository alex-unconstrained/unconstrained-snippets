UnconstrainED · Workshop for the Veracity team

Claude at Work, two afternoons

Facilitated by Alex Johnson (UnconstrainED) with Gavan Porter (GSP Advisory)

A hands-on workshop for the finance and bookkeeping team, run as two afternoons a week apart. Day 1 makes Claude's output trustworthy. Day 2 makes it repeatable. In the week between, you run one of your own processes through Claude and log how it goes, so the skill you build on Day 2 comes from your real work, not a demo.

Day 1 · Make it trustworthy

2h45
  1. 0:00–0:15
    Opening

    Each person names the one process they most want off their plate. Why we're doing this: AI working alongside a person, with the person still accountable for the result.

  2. 0:15–1:00
    Prompting and Projects for finance work

    Set up a Claude Project holding the context you re-type every time (client templates, chart-of-accounts conventions, import formats) plus custom instructions. Prompt patterns that suit bookkeeping.

  3. 1:00–1:40
    Checking without re-doing

    The core block. Review gates, checking by sampling, getting Claude to show its reasoning and flag low-confidence rows, and deciding where a person signs off. Worked live on mapping invoices to an import template. Includes a short segment on client data: what stays out of prompts.

  4. 1:40–1:50
    Break
  5. 1:50–2:30
    Artifacts, tracking sheets, Claude in Excel vs Copilot

    Build a structured tracking sheet as an artifact. Then run the same task in Claude in Excel and in Copilot side by side, to see which tool suits which job.

  6. 2:30–2:45
    Set the week

    Each person commits to one process: at least three runs through Claude, the review gate every time, accuracy and time saved logged in the tracking sheet.

Between the days · about a week

Your task

  1. Run your chosen process through the Day 1 Project at least three times on live work.
  2. Log each run: accuracy, what was wrong, how long the check took, time saved.
  3. Bring your best prompt and 2–3 real examples to Day 2, including at least one where Claude got it wrong. The failures teach the most.

There may be a short mid-week check-in if you get stuck.

Day 2 · Make it repeatable

2h45
  1. 0:00–0:20
    What the week showed

    Each person walks through their log: where Claude held up, where it broke, what the review step caught.

  2. 0:20–1:05
    Cowork and routines

    Hand off multi-step work in Cowork on your real files. Set up a routine for a recurring task such as weekly reconciliation prep or a month-end checklist.

  3. 1:05–1:15
    Break
  4. 1:15–2:15
    Capstone: build your skill

    Each person turns their process into a skill with the review step written into it. Work happens in small breakout groups with a facilitator in each. Connectors only where they link to tools you already use.

  5. 2:15–2:35
    Test and showcase

    Run each skill on a fresh real example and check it in front of the group. The test question: would you send this to a client?

  6. 2:35–2:45
    Close

    Who looks after each skill, the next process to take on, and a 30-day check-in.

Before Day 1

  1. Pick one process you repeat every week and would happily hand off. Invoice-to-import mapping, reconciliation prep and month-end checklists are all good candidates.
  2. Gather 2–3 examples of that process with client details removed or replaced: an input, the finished output, and any template you use.
  3. Check your access. Make sure you can sign in to Claude on the account you'll use at work, and have Excel open if you use it.
  4. Join from a laptop with a second screen if you have one. Every block is hands-on.
