Delivery details

How the programme works

Use Little Nest's tasks and approved material, try the tools between sessions, then bring back what needs fixing. Lead teachers may join Session 3 and the opening hour of Session 1 at no extra fee.

Three on-site sessions in Mumbai

Session 1 / Foundations and scheduling / 3 hours

Practise prompting, set up recurring work in Claude Projects, agree data rules and start the scheduling assistant. Identify an owner for each tool. Lead teachers may join the opening hour.

Session 2 / Data and the weekly report / 3 hours

Work with bookings, attendance and feedback; refine the schedule against a real week; build a repeatable supervisor report for human review.

Session 3 / Notes and living documents / 3 hours

Turn suitable notes into drafts, set up the Microsoft 365 workflow and create teaching resources. Lead teachers join this session. Hand over the tools and prompts, and draft a short explanation of AI use for Little Nest to approve before sharing with parents.

Six online office hours

Six 60-minute calls between Round 1 and April 2027, at times agreed across Mumbai and the U.S. Any team member can bring real work or questions. Two calls are reserved by default for the tools needing most support.

Boundaries and progress

A person reviews everything before it is sent. Safeguarding, decisions about children, staff assessment and childcare compliance stay outside the programme. Free accounts are for generic teaching material only, with no identifying child, family or staff data. These tools will not monitor or score staff.

Record a baseline in Session 1. By April, review time saved, eight consecutive weekly reports, one full scheduling cycle and regular independent use by at least four of the seven participants.

Dates to agree together

Before we start

  1. Agree the three dates, seven participants, seat count and lead-teacher attendance.
  2. Name the Microsoft 365 approver and test permissions two weeks before Session 1. Activate Claude seats one week before.
  3. Share the supervisor-report template and name a data-hygiene owner. Choose the four tool owners in Session 1.

If Microsoft 365 approval is delayed, start with approved copies and move the living-document setup to an office hour.
